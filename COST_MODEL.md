# Cost Model

## Primary normalized scenario
The strategy is evaluated as one 65-unit NIFTY lot equivalent for all historical trades. This keeps the P&L series comparable across historical lot-size regimes; the report does not claim that 65 was the historical lot size in every year.

Six option-leg executions are assumed per round trip: three at entry and three at expiry exit.

Current statutory/broker cost inputs used in the primary cost-equivalent scenario:
- Paytm Money F&O brokerage: ₹20 per executed order.
- NSE equity-options transaction charge: ₹3,553 per crore of premium value per side from 1 March 2026.
- STT on sale of options: 0.15% of option premium from 1 April 2026.
- Stamp duty on equity options: 0.003% on buyer-side consideration.
- SEBI turnover fee: ₹10 per crore of traded value.
- GST: 18% on broker/exchange service components.
- Slippage: one option tick (₹0.05) adverse per execution; two-tick sensitivity.

The baseline therefore emphasizes a current-cost-equivalent comparison over reconstructing every historical tariff change. A zero-cost control is also reported.
